# Aarvya Realty — SEO Strategy & Optimization Guide

## 1. Targeted Keywords (Pune & Maharashtra)

### Primary High-Intent Keywords
- `Real estate in Pune`
- `Flats for sale in Pune`
- `2 BHK flats in Pune`
- `3 BHK apartments in Baner`
- `Properties in Wakad`
- `Flats in Kharadi`
- `Property consultants in Pune`
- `Residential properties in Maharashtra`
- `Commercial properties in Pune`
- `Hinjewadi real estate`

### Micro-Market Specific Keywords
- **Baner / Balewadi:** Luxury residences, Balewadi High Street proximity, hill view apartments.
- **Hinjawadi (Phase 1, 2, 3):** Walk-to-work IT housing, rental yield investments, ready-to-move flats near Infosys/Wipro circle.
- **Kharadi:** Eastern IT corridor homes, EON IT Park apartments, high rental yield properties.
- **Wakad:** Family apartments, Bhumkar Chowk connectivity, Datta Mandir road developments.
- **Viman Nagar:** Commercial Grade-A offices, proximity to Pune International Airport.

## 2. Technical SEO Elements Implemented

- **Semantic HTML5:** Proper use of `<header>`, `<main>`, `<article>`, `<section>`, `<footer>`, and heading hierarchy (`h1`, `h2`, `h3`).
- **Open Graph & Twitter Cards:** Pre-configured social sharing previews in `index.html`.
- **Structured Sitemaps:** Fully indexed XML sitemap at `public/sitemap.xml` covering all main routes and property categories.
- **Robots.txt:** Standard crawl directives at `public/robots.txt`.
- **Mobile First Indexing:** Viewport tags, responsive typography scaling from 360px mobile viewports up to 4K displays.
- **Performance:** Clean code-splitting with Vite, sub-6 second build times, lazy loading on all property and city card photographs.

---

## 3. Google Search Console & Analytics Integration

To connect Google Search Console:
1. Go to [Google Search Console](https://search.google.com/search-console).
2. Add your domain property (`aarvyarealty.in`).
3. Verify ownership via DNS TXT record or HTML meta tag in `client/index.html`.
4. Submit sitemap URL: `https://aarvyarealty.in/sitemap.xml`.
